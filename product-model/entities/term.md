# Term

A tag or a category as it exists across the items that carry it. Terms written differently that share an address — Go, go and GO — are one term, called the way most items write it.

## Information kept

- **Name** — how most items write it
- **Taxonomy** — tags or categories
- **Other ways of writing** — how else items write it
- **Count** — how many items carry it
- **Address** — its page on the site, such as /tags/go/
